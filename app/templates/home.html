CHAIN OF CUSTODY & AUDIT

Forensic Rigor & Defense-in-Depth

Cryptographic Evidence Integrity

Every piece of ingested digital evidence—whether a scanned FIR, an Excel transaction ledger, or a surveillance image—is SHA-256 hashed upon upload. The hash is recorded in a secure database registry and verified during investigations.

  • ✓ Automatic SHA-256 fingerprinting on ingestion
  • ✓ On-demand integrity verification against disk files
  • ✓ Tamper detection with status flags

Hash-Linked Audit Ledger

Investigative actions, case updates, document uploads, and security events are committed to a local cryptographic hash chain. Each block references its parent block hash, guaranteeing tamper-evident audit trails.

  • ✓ Immutable event payload storage
  • ✓ Genesis-to-tip hash continuity checks
  • ✓ Exportable audit trail for judicial proceedings
